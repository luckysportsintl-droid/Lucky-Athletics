# Template provenance and prompts

The built-in image-generation tool (not the API/CLI fallback) created two neutral transparent atlases from the supplied `public/images/catalog-1.webp` and `catalog-2.webp` references. The project assets are `public/templates/products-1.png` and `products-2.png`. All individual design patterns are applied deterministically by project code, not generated separately by AI.

## Atlas 1 prompt

Use case: precise-object-edit. Asset type: neutral product template atlas for pattern texture compositing. Edit this reference image. Preserve exactly its 4 columns by 3 rows with the SAME 12 items in the SAME positions, each item entirely within its cell. Preserve highly photorealistic 3D clothing folds, shapes, sports cuts, fabric weave, seams, pockets and garment details. Remove ALL red, black and white graphic stripes or printed designs; make ALL printable fabric surfaces uniformly neutral light silver-gray / off-white, with strong realistic grayscale fabric folds and highlights. Keep bag handles, zippers, buckles, drawstrings and nonprintable hardware dark charcoal. Clothes must be entirely blank gray/white, no dark collars, no dark stripes. Remove the studio background to true transparency, including spaces between items and inside handles and between jersey and shorts. No floor shadows or background panels. Row1: basketball sleeveless jersey and shorts; cheer sleeveless crop top and pleated skirt; padded American football jersey and knee length football pants; soccer short sleeve jersey and shorts. Row2: lacrosse mesh tank and shorts; track running singlet and short shorts; long sleeve pullover hoodie; long sleeve zip jacket. Row3: backpack; drawstring bag; duffel bag; tote bag. Clean ghost-mannequin product photography; exact original 4x3 equal-cell registration, front view, no overlap. Landscape 1536x1024 atlas, high resolution.

## Atlas 2 prompt

Use case: precise-object-edit. Asset type: neutral product template atlas for pattern texture compositing. Edit the supplied image. Preserve EXACTLY the 4 columns x 3 rows, SAME 12 categories in SAME equal cells. Make ALL printable clothing fabric blank neutral light silver gray / off-white. Remove all original red/black/white printed graphics stripes color blocking. Strong realistic grayscale folds, hems, seams, woven fabric and 3D volume. Keep zips, buttons and belts charcoal, but all collars and fabric blank light gray. Remove studio background to genuine alpha transparency: empty gaps between items, between legs, around shapes. No background shadows no panels. Row1 baseball button shirt with trousers; softball button jersey with trousers; fitted volleyball jersey and shorts; long sleeve hockey jersey. Row2 wrestling singlet (one continuous garment); rugby polo shirt and shorts; cricket polo shirt and long trousers; tennis polo and pleated skirt. Row3 cycling zip jersey and fitted padded shorts; esports short sleeve jersey only; tracksuit zip jacket and trousers; training tee and shorts. For categories with extra detached trousers in original, simplify to ONE top plus ONE pair of bottoms within same cell; remove duplicate spare bottoms. Exact sports cuts, ghost mannequin realistic front-view e-commerce photography. No text, logos or color designs. Landscape 1536x1024 atlas.

## Output review

The atlases have transparent backgrounds and usable neutral fabric detail. Some categories retain the original reference's extra detached bottoms; those show an additional product view rather than a distinct design. The deterministic renderer removes tiny isolated image residue before generating the final product previews. Templates should be replaced with exact production product photography if exact cuts, stitching or panel registration are required.
